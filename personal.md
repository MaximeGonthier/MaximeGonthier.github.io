## Visiting U.S. National Parks
While living in Chicago I got hooked on the American national parks. They really are <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_National_Parks:_America%27s_Best_Idea">America's best idea</a>: they protect unique landscapes and biodiversity, and they stay open to everyone.
My favorite is Mount Rainier by a wide margin, for the hikes surrounded by wildflowers and groundhogs. Then come Zion, Yellowstone and Arches. Below is a pin for every park I've visited so far: hover one for its name.

## Games Done Quick Commentary
Games Done Quick is a nonprofit that runs speedrunning marathons to raise money for charity, gathering hundreds of players who finish games as fast as they can.
To make the event accessible to non-English speakers, a French restream commentates the runs every year, and I was lucky enough to be picked as a commentator for a few editions:
- SGDQ 2023
- AGDQ 2020
- SGDQ 2019
- AGDQ 2019
- SGDQ 2018
- SGDQ 2017

## Summer Camp Leadership
As a student I worked as a summer camp leader, organizing outdoor activities, team challenges and creative workshops for kids.
I did this at camps run by IGESA, AVEA, Wakanga and Djuringa, and at primary schools in Elancourt and Versailles during the summer holidays.
